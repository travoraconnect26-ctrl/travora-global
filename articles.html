A practical pre-booking check

Before paying for a trip, independently confirm the details that can create the biggest problems if they are wrong.

1Dates and names: Check dates, passenger names and passport validity against the booking requirements.
2Entry permission: Verify visa, transit and entry rules with the destination's official authorities.
3Total price: Include taxes, baggage, transfers, deposits, fees and cancellation costs.
4Provider details: Confirm the official provider, property address, contact details and payment terms.
5Transport connections: Allow realistic transfer time and check the current operator schedule.
6Contingency: Keep copies of key documents, emergency contacts and a reserve for disruption.
