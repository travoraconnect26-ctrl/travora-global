Switzerland Travel Guide

Practical starting notes for US and UK visitors.

Key destinations
Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken and Geneva
Best months
June–September for hiking; winter sports in colder months.
Currency & tipping
Swiss franc (CHF); service is included.
