Spain Travel Guide

Spain's autonomous communities have distinct languages, histories and food cultures. The experience of Catalonia, Andalusia, the Basque Country, Galicia, Madrid and the islands is not interchangeable.

Spain's autonomous communities have distinct languages, histories and food cultures. The experience of Catalonia, Andalusia, the Basque Country, Galicia, Madrid and the islands is not interchangeable.

Choose the right regions

Madrid is a strong base for museums, parks and central rail connections. Catalonia combines Barcelona with coastal and mountain destinations. Andalusia brings cities such as Seville, Córdoba and Granada, each with its own architectural and cultural layers. The Basque Country is known for its food culture and green coast, while Galicia offers Atlantic landscapes and a different culinary tradition.

Travel between cities

High-speed rail links many major cities, but not every route is direct and smaller towns may require regional trains or buses. Compare rail and coach schedules before fixing accommodation. In cities, public transport is usually more practical than a rental car; a car can help when exploring rural areas.

Food and regional identity

Try local specialities rather than expecting one national menu: pintxos in the Basque Country, seafood in Galicia, Andalusian tapas traditions, Catalan dishes and central Spanish cooking all differ. Be aware that meal times may be later than visitors are used to, and regional languages are part of everyday life in several areas.

Season and pace

Summer can be very hot inland and busy on popular coasts. Spring and autumn can suit city touring and walking, though local conditions vary. Build a slower pace into large cities and allow time for neighbourhoods beyond the main monuments.

A first itinerary

A week could focus on Madrid with either Andalusia or Barcelona, depending on interests and transport connections. For a longer trip, add a distinct region rather than attempting every major city in one circuit.

Before you go

Check current entry conditions, rail operator schedules, local festival dates and any regional transport disruptions. Book popular attractions through official ticket channels.

Travel information: Entry rules, prices, opening times, safety conditions, transport schedules and access can change. Confirm details with official government, transport and destination sources before departure.
