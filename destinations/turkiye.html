Türkiye Travel Guide

Türkiye connects Europe and West Asia and contains a wide range of regions: Istanbul's layered urban history, Aegean and Mediterranean coastlines, central Anatolian landscapes, Black Sea highlands and eastern cultural centres.

Türkiye connects Europe and West Asia and contains a wide range of regions: Istanbul's layered urban history, Aegean and Mediterranean coastlines, central Anatolian landscapes, Black Sea highlands and eastern cultural centres.

Where to focus

Istanbul offers historic neighbourhoods, mosques, museums, markets and ferry journeys between continents. Cappadocia is known for distinctive geological landscapes and cave architecture. The Aegean coast includes archaeological sites and seaside towns, while the Mediterranean coast offers beaches and access to historic routes. The Black Sea region has greener mountain scenery and a different climate.

Moving around

Domestic flights can save time across long distances; intercity buses serve many destinations, and rail is useful on selected corridors. Istanbul traffic can be heavy, so use metro, tram, ferry and other public transport where practical. For rural areas, confirm transfer details and return options before booking accommodation.

Food and hospitality

Regional food ranges from breakfast spreads and grilled dishes to vegetable-based Aegean cooking, Black Sea specialities and southeastern cuisine. Ask about ingredients if you have allergies, and be respectful when visiting religious sites or traditional neighbourhoods.

Season and outdoor plans

Coastal summers can be hot and busy, while spring and autumn may suit archaeological touring and walking. Cappadocia and mountain areas have their own seasonal conditions. Check local weather and operator guidance before booking balloon flights, hikes or boat excursions.

A balanced first trip

For a first visit, combine Istanbul with one other region such as Cappadocia or the Aegean coast. Distances are substantial, so avoid adding several far-apart areas to a short holiday.

Before you go

Entry rules vary by nationality and can change. Verify requirements through official Turkish government sources, and check current transport, site-access and weather information.
