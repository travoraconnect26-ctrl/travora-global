Thailand Travel Guide

Thailand offers a varied journey through Bangkok's urban energy, northern cultural and mountain landscapes, historic former capitals and southern coasts and islands. The best route depends on the season and which side of the peninsula you choose; the Andaman coast and Gulf coast do not share identical weather patterns.

Where to go

Bangkok combines temples, markets, museums, river transport and modern neighbourhoods. Northern Thailand offers Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai, mountain scenery and craft traditions. The Andaman side includes Phuket, Krabi and nearby islands; the Gulf side includes Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao. Ayutthaya is a useful historic excursion from Bangkok.

Choosing a coast and season

Compare conditions for your exact travel dates before choosing an island. Sea state, rainfall and marine activity vary by coast and month. Check local marine forecasts, national-park notices and ferry operator updates; do not assume a boat will run simply because a timetable is published.

Getting around

Bangkok has rail transit, buses, taxis and river boats. Intercity journeys are possible by train, coach and domestic flight. Island transfers require ferries or speedboats; confirm the departure pier, operator, luggage limits and connection time. Avoid tight links between separate tickets and allow recovery time after overnight travel.

Food and culture

Explore regional dishes such as northern khao soi, northeastern som tam and larb, central curries and southern seafood. Tell vendors about allergies and ask about ingredients such as fish sauce, shrimp paste and peanuts. At temples, dress respectfully, follow posted rules and be considerate of worshippers.

Responsible experiences

Choose wildlife experiences that prioritise animal welfare and avoid riding, performances or direct-contact attractions. Use licensed operators for diving and boat trips, respect marine protected areas, reduce single-use plastic and follow local guidance around reefs and wildlife.

A realistic first itinerary

For a week, combine Bangkok with either the north or one coast, rather than trying to cover both coasts and several islands. For a longer visit, add a second region only when travel time and seasonal conditions make sense.

Before you go

Visa rules depend on nationality and may change. Confirm entry conditions with official Thai immigration and embassy sources. Check current weather warnings, marine conditions, national-park closures and transport schedules before each leg.

Travel information: Entry rules, prices, opening times, safety conditions, transport schedules and access can change. Confirm details with official government, transport and destination sources before departure.
