The World. One Journey.

TRAVORA brings worldwide travel discovery into one free, English-language place.

What you can explore

Start with country reference profiles, explore cities and populated places, browse destination guides, discover islands and attractions, and use the interactive map to understand where places are in the world. Build a personal itinerary with the trip planner and continue to external providers when you want to research travel options.

One connected world

Our goal is to make it easier to move between countries, regions, cities, smaller settlements and points of interest without treating famous destinations as the whole world. Coverage is growing and is not yet exhaustive: some datasets focus on populated places above a population threshold, and selected editorial guides cover only a subset of destinations.

Free for travellers

Responsible discovery

Travel information can change. Check official government, transport and destination sources for current entry requirements, safety advice, schedules and access conditions before making decisions.

Explore all country profiles, cities and towns, destination guides or start with the world map.

Dataset coverage, sources and limitations

The country directory covers 195 countries using the common international count (193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine). Country cards include reference capitals, native names, currency names and codes, languages and calling codes. The interactive world map contains 34,480 unique locations: 195 country reference points, 34,248 populated places, 15 island locations and 22 attractions. City markers load as visitors zoom, search or choose the Cities filter. The Worldwide Cities Directory lets visitors search 34,248 populated places by name, country and region, with administrative areas, coordinates, time zones, population references and map links. The Detailed Guides library contains 253 editorial destination profiles covering regional geography, signature experiences, food culture, transport, trip planning and responsible travel. These 253 guides are curated in-depth profiles, not a claim that every city or attraction has its own full editorial guide. TRAVORA is a travel discovery and planning website, not the merchant or booking provider. Some search tools connect visitors to third-party travel providers through Travelpayouts. Providers control their live results, prices, availability, booking terms and payment process; TRAVORA does not process reservations or payments. Country names, capitals, currencies and languages are adapted from the Annexare Countries dataset (MIT licence). Population, area, subregion, timezone and centroid fields include adapted data from the Countries States Cities Database (ODbL 1.0, including share-alike obligations). Populated-place data is from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). See data sources and licences. All are static reference snapshots, not official government advice. Always confirm travel details with the relevant provider.
