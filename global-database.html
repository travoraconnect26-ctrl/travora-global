How TRAVORA handles data

Each imported record should retain its source, source identifier, licence, retrieval date, country, coordinates where available, and a confidence or verification status. Passenger statistics must include the reporting organisation, reporting period, unit and definition. Annual passenger throughput is not the same as individual passenger profiles. TRAVORA will not collect private passenger identities or personal travel histories.

Open map and directory data can help identify places and facilities, but they do not automatically provide every operator's full routes, current fleet, room inventory, ticket prices or passenger totals. Those need separate official or licensed sources. Data coverage and missing fields must remain visible.
