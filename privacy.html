Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

TRAVORA is an independent travel discovery website. This page explains the current website features and the types of information that may be processed when you use them. This is a general website notice, not legal advice. The operator should review it for the laws that apply to the countries where the website is used.

Information you provide

The current public discovery pages do not ask you to create an account to browse countries, places or guides. The trip planner can store itinerary details in your browser's local storage when you use its save feature. That information stays in the browser on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Do not enter passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information into trip notes.

Technical data and hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, requested pages and request time to deliver and protect the service. TRAVORA does not currently operate its own advertising profile or analytics database.

Maps, images and external websites

Some features load map tiles, libraries, images or place data from third-party services. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive technical connection data under its own privacy policy. Links to booking, map, transport or other external websites take you away from TRAVORA; their privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Advertising

TRAVORA's planned revenue model is display advertising only. Advertising is not currently activated on this website. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads as permitted by the user's choices and applicable law. This policy and the site's consent controls will be updated before advertising is enabled. For information about Google's advertising practices, visit Google's advertising and cookie information.

Your choices and questions

You can clear browser local storage to remove trip planner data saved on that device. You can also use external services directly and review their privacy notices. For website questions or privacy requests, use the TRAVORA contact page.

Changes

This notice may be updated when website features, hosting or advertising arrangements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
