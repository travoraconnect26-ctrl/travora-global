Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

TRAVORA is an independent travel discovery website. This page explains the current website features and the types of information that may be processed when you use them. This is a general website notice, not legal advice. The operator should review it for the laws that apply to the countries where the website is used.

Information you provide

The current public discovery pages do not ask you to create an account to browse countries, places or guides. The trip planner can store itinerary details in your browser's local storage when you use its save feature. Destination guides also store a short list of recently viewed destination names in local storage so the homepage can offer a continue-exploring shortcut. These records stay in that browser on your device and are not uploaded to TRAVORA's servers. You can clear recent destinations from the homepage or clear browser local storage. Do not enter passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information into trip notes.

Technical data and hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, requested pages and request time to deliver and protect the service. TRAVORA uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HL59SJE1TW) on selected pages to measure page views and interaction events such as destination searches, comparison searches, destination links and outbound provider clicks. Google processes this measurement data under its own terms and privacy documentation; TRAVORA does not operate a separate analytics database.

Maps, images and external websites

Some features load map tiles, libraries, images or place data from third-party services. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive technical connection data under its own privacy policy. Links to booking, map, transport or other external websites take you away from TRAVORA; their privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Affiliate links and commissions

Some links to travel providers are affiliate links. If you follow one of these links and later make a qualifying purchase or booking, TRAVORA may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links may contain tracking identifiers so the provider or affiliate network can attribute a referral. The provider handles its own availability, prices, booking, payment, fulfilment, cancellations and customer support under its terms and privacy policy. TRAVORA does not receive or control the payment you make to that provider.

Advertising

TRAVORA plans to earn advertising revenue through Google AdSense only. A Google AdSense script is present on some pages. Depending on Google account approval and settings, Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising. Google may process information such as browser or device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions under its own terms and policies. The website operator must verify AdSense approval and configure any consent choices required for the locations and advertising settings served before enabling or expanding ad serving. For information about Google's advertising practices, visit Google's advertising and cookie information.

Your choices and questions

You can clear recent destinations from the homepage or clear browser local storage to remove locally saved trip planner and destination history data. You can also use external services directly and review their privacy notices. For website questions or privacy requests, use the TRAVORA contact page.

Changes

This notice may be updated when website features, affiliate partners, hosting, analytics or advertising arrangements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
