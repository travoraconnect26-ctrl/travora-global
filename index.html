About TRAVORA
TRAVORA is an independent travel discovery project being developed to help travelers explore destinations and find useful travel options through established booking partners.

Affiliate disclosure: Some links may eventually be affiliate links. If you book through an approved partner link, TRAVORA may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Important: The country directory covers 195 countries using the common international count (193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine). The directory also includes a curated selection of cities, islands and attractions. Our separate Detailed Guides library now contains 54 expanded destination profiles covering regional geography, signature experiences, food culture, transport, itinerary planning and responsible travel. Coverage is still being expanded and is not yet a complete guide to every destination. This is an informational directory, not a live booking service. Live prices, availability, reservations and payments are not currently connected. Always confirm travel details with the relevant provider.
