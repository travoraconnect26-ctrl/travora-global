About TRAVORA
TRAVORA is an independent travel discovery and planning website. Use our destination guides, city and country directories, interactive map and travel search tools to research your next journey.

Affiliate disclosure: TRAVORA uses Travelpayouts booking tools and selected affiliate links, including Klook and Yesim. If you use an eligible partner tool or link and complete a qualifying booking or purchase, TRAVORA may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change our goal of providing useful travel information. Search results, prices, availability and booking terms are controlled by the relevant travel provider.

Important: The country directory covers 195 countries using the common international count (193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine). Country cards include reference capitals, native names, currency names and codes, languages and calling codes. The interactive world map contains 34,480 unique locations: 195 country reference points, 34,248 populated places, 15 island locations and 22 attractions. City markers load as visitors zoom, search or choose the Cities filter. The Worldwide Cities Directory lets visitors search 34,248 populated places by name, country and region, with administrative areas, coordinates, time zones, population references and map links. The Detailed Guides library contains 253 editorial destination profiles covering regional geography, signature experiences, food culture, transport, trip planning and responsible travel. These 253 guides are curated in-depth profiles, not a claim that every city or attraction has its own full editorial guide. TRAVORA is a travel discovery and planning website, not the merchant or booking provider. Some search tools connect visitors to third-party travel providers through Travelpayouts. Providers control their live results, prices, availability, booking terms and payment process; TRAVORA does not process reservations or payments. Country names, capitals, currencies and languages are adapted from the Annexare Countries dataset (MIT licence). Population, area, subregion, timezone and centroid fields include adapted data from the Countries States Cities Database (ODbL 1.0, including share-alike obligations). Populated-place data is from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). See data sources and licences. All are static reference snapshots, not official government advice. Always confirm travel details with the relevant provider.
