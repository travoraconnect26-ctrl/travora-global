Free Travel Itinerary Template

Organize your trip in one place. Add your travel dates, flights, accommodation, daily activities and important notes, then print the plan or save it as a PDF.

A useful travel itinerary is more than a list of attractions. It keeps confirmed bookings, transfer details and daily plans together while leaving enough flexibility for delays, weather and discoveries along the way. This template works for a weekend break, a multi-city holiday or a longer international trip.

4. Budget and booking checklist

  • Check passport validity and entry rules for your nationality.
  • Confirm baggage, check-in and cancellation rules with each provider.
  • Save accommodation addresses and essential bookings for offline access.
  • Check local transport timetables and airport transfer times.
  • Keep emergency contacts and insurance details accessible.
