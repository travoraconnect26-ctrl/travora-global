International eSIMs

Carrier international day passes can cost around $10–$12 per day, depending on your plan and destination. Compare an eSIM before relying on roaming.

Flight delay compensation

Some US–EU and US–UK itineraries may qualify under European or UK passenger-rights rules. Eligible claims can be up to €600; conditions and carrier/route rules apply.
