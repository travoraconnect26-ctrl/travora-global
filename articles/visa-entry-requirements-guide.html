Travel Visa Guide: Research Entry Rules Before You Book

A careful process for checking visa, passport, transit and entry requirements using official government sources.

TRAVORA Editorial · Updated 26 November 2026
Home / Travel articles
Important: This is a general planning guide, not live travel information or individual legal, medical or insurance advice. Entry rules, prices, availability, schedules and safety conditions change. Confirm current details with official authorities and providers before booking or departure.

Start with official government sources

Use the destination country's immigration department, embassy or consulate website. Check whether you need a visa, electronic authorisation, visa on arrival or another permission. Confirm permitted activities, maximum stay, number of entries and the date by which you must enter. Avoid relying only on social media posts or third-party summaries.

Check passport and supporting documents

Review passport validity, blank-page requirements and any rules for children travelling with one parent or without parents. Some applications ask for accommodation details, return or onward travel, funds, invitation letters or proof of employment. Requirements differ, so follow the current official checklist for your case.

Review transit and changing rules

A transit through another country may require permission even when you do not plan to leave the airport, depending on nationality, airport, terminal change and ticket arrangement. Check every transit point. Apply through the official channel with enough time, use accurate information and keep copies of approvals. Recheck requirements before departure because rules can change.
