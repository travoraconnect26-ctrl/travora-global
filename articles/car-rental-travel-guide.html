Car Rental Travel Guide: Compare Providers, Insurance and Driving Rules

Plan a rental-car trip by checking licence rules, insurance excess, deposits, fuel policies and local driving laws.

TRAVORA Editorial · Updated 26 November 2026
Important: This is a general planning guide, not live travel information or individual legal, medical or insurance advice. Entry rules, prices, availability, schedules and safety conditions change. Confirm current details with official authorities and providers before booking or departure.

Check eligibility and local rules

Confirm the minimum age, licence duration, accepted licence format and whether an International Driving Permit or translation is required. Rules depend on the destination and your licence-issuing country. Check road signs, toll systems, low-emission zones, child-seat requirements and whether your route crosses borders.

Understand insurance and charges

Read the collision damage waiver, theft protection, liability cover and excess amount. Ask what is excluded, including tyres, glass, roof, underbody or off-road use. Check the deposit, accepted payment cards, additional-driver fees, young-driver fees, mileage limits, fuel policy and one-way charges. Do not assume the insurance from a payment card applies to every rental.

Inspect and return the vehicle

Photograph existing damage, fuel level, mileage and the vehicle's condition at pickup; make sure damage is recorded on the rental agreement. Learn the emergency process and keep the provider's contact details. Return the car on time, follow the fuel policy and keep the final receipt. Never drive if local conditions, fatigue or unfamiliar roads make it unsafe.
