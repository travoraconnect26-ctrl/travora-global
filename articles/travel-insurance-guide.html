Travel Insurance Guide: Understand Medical, Cancellation and Baggage Cover

Learn how to compare travel insurance cover, exclusions, claims processes and activity-specific protection.

TRAVORA Editorial · Updated 26 November 2026
Home / Travel articles
Important: This is a general planning guide, not live travel information or individual legal, medical or insurance advice. Entry rules, prices, availability, schedules and safety conditions change. Confirm current details with official authorities and providers before booking or departure.

Identify the cover you need

Consider emergency medical treatment, medical evacuation, trip cancellation or interruption, travel delays, baggage loss and personal liability. Your needs may differ if you have connecting flights, expensive prepaid bookings, valuable equipment, a cruise or remote activities. Check whether the policy covers the full trip duration and all destinations.

Read exclusions and conditions

Look closely at pre-existing medical condition rules, age limits, alcohol-related exclusions, unattended baggage, high-risk activities, government travel warnings and supplier insolvency. Some activities require an add-on or specialist policy. Check excess amounts, sub-limits, claim deadlines and whether you must contact the insurer before arranging treatment.

Know how to make a claim

Save the policy certificate and emergency assistance number offline. Keep receipts, booking confirmations, medical reports and written evidence of delays or cancellations. Contact the insurer promptly when the policy requires it and follow the claims process. Insurance does not replace sensible precautions or official travel advice.
