Cruise Holiday Guide: Choose Ships, Routes and Shore Days

Understand cruise itineraries, cabin choices, port days, shore excursions and the extra costs to check.

TRAVORA Editorial · Updated 26 November 2026
Home / Travel articles
Important: This is a general planning guide, not live travel information or individual legal, medical or insurance advice. Entry rules, prices, availability, schedules and safety conditions change. Confirm current details with official authorities and providers before booking or departure.

Compare itinerary and ship

Look at the ports, arrival and departure times, number of sea days and the amount of time available in each destination. Check the ship's size, accessibility, dining arrangements, onboard activities and suitability for your group. Port calls can change because of weather or operational decisions, so treat the published route as subject to the cruise contract.

Calculate the real cost

Review what the fare includes and what costs extra: gratuities, drinks, speciality dining, internet, excursions, transfers, taxes and service charges. Compare cabin locations and understand the difference between inside, ocean-view, balcony and suite categories. Read cancellation terms and payment deadlines before booking.

Prepare for embarkation and shore visits

Check passport validity, visa and transit requirements for every port, not only the embarkation country. Confirm the cruise line's document deadlines and arrival time. For independent shore visits, plan a realistic return buffer and understand the ship's all-aboard time. Consider medical needs, motion sensitivity and travel insurance that specifically covers cruise travel and relevant activities.
