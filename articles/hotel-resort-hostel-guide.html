Choosing Accommodation: Hotels, Resorts, Hostels and Holiday Rentals

Compare accommodation types by location, facilities, guest needs, policies and total cost.

TRAVORA Editorial · Updated 26 November 2026
Home / Travel articles
Important: This is a general planning guide, not live travel information or individual legal, medical or insurance advice. Entry rules, prices, availability, schedules and safety conditions change. Confirm current details with official authorities and providers before booking or departure.

Choose the accommodation type

Hotels can suit travellers who value reception services, daily housekeeping and predictable facilities. Resorts may bundle leisure amenities and meals but can be far from local communities. Hostels can offer lower prices and shared social spaces, with varying privacy and security. Holiday rentals may provide kitchens and more room, but check local rules, host responsibilities and service arrangements.

Compare location and total price

Map the property against transport, planned activities, shops and late-night arrival routes. Read recent reviews for recurring issues such as noise, cleanliness, maintenance and communication. Compare taxes, cleaning fees, deposits, resort fees, breakfast and cancellation conditions rather than relying on the first displayed price.

Confirm important details directly

Ask the property to confirm check-in times, luggage storage, accessibility, child policies, extra beds, parking and any special requirements. Use secure payment methods and be cautious if asked to pay outside the booking platform's protected process. Save the address, contact details and reservation terms offline before travelling.
